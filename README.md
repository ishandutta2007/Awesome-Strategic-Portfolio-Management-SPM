## Top Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Strategic Alignment, Portfolio Prioritization & Enterprise Project Governance*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM)**. These tools enable organizations to connect strategy to execution—prioritizing investments, balancing resources across initiatives, and governing project portfolios to ensure every effort aligns with business objectives.



**Examples** include ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management, Planview, Broadcom Clarity, Jira Align, Shibumi, Meisterplan, Workfront, Apptio Targetprocess, Cora PPM, and Sciforma (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosted portfolio management, strategy-to-execution alignment, and resource-constrained planning—ideal for PMOs, enterprises, and public sector organizations building sovereign portfolio governance without per-seat licensing lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Enterprise SPM suite (formerly ITBM) covering demand management, project portfolio management, resource management, financial planning, and innovation management; activated via modular plugins on the Now Platform with SPM and SPM Pro editions .



- **[Planview](https://www.planview.com/)**  

  Comprehensive portfolio management platform spanning strategic planning, work management, and resource capacity planning for enterprise PMOs.



- **[Broadcom Clarity](https://www.broadcom.com/)**  

  Enterprise PPM platform (formerly CA Clarity) with portfolio planning, financial governance, and resource management for large IT and transformation portfolios.



- **[Jira Align](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/align)**  

  Enterprise agile planning platform connecting strategy to execution across teams, with portfolio-level visibility and SAFe support.



- **[Shibumi](https://www.shibumi.com/)**  

  Strategic portfolio management platform with scenario modeling, prioritization scoring, and executive dashboards for strategic initiative governance.



- **[Meisterplan](https://meisterplan.com/)**  

  Lean portfolio management tool focused on resource capacity planning and scenario simulation to balance demand against capacity.



- **[Workfront](https://www.workfront.com/)**  

  Adobe's work management platform with portfolio management, resource planning, and project governance capabilities for enterprise teams.



- **[Apptio Targetprocess](https://www.apptio.com/products/targetprocess/)**  

  Agile portfolio management platform with lean budgeting, value stream management, and SAFe-aligned portfolio planning.



- **[Cora PPM](https://www.corappm.com/)**  

  Project and portfolio management system with demand management, resource planning, and strategic alignment for PMOs.



- **[Sciforma](https://www.sciforma.com/)**  

  PPM and work management platform with portfolio prioritization, resource capacity planning, and stage-gate governance.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  

  The leading open-source project management software (GPL-3.0) with comprehensive portfolio management features: project and portfolio overviews, Gantt charts with cross-project dependencies, budgets and cost reporting, time tracking, agile boards (Kanban/Scrum/SAFe), roadmaps, and a PMflex-aligned portfolio structure for public sector governance; available as free Community Edition (self-hosted) or paid Enterprise editions .



- **[Leantime](https://github.com/Leantime/leantime)**  

  Strategy-first open-source project management system (MIT) designed for non-project managers; includes goal cascading with OKRs, a Goal Matrix for effort-vs-impact prioritization, portfolio dashboards showing alignment against strategic pillars, and cross-project program views introduced in v3.9.7; community edition is free for unlimited users when self-hosted .



- **[Odoo (Project Module)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP with a Project module that can be extended for portfolio management via community modules like "Project Portfolio" and "Resourcing"; integrates project data with accounting, HR, and CRM for financial visibility per project; Community edition is free and self-hosted, with portfolio features requiring assembly of modules .



- **[Redmine](https://github.com/redmine/redmine)**  

  Veteran open-source project management tool (GPL-2.0) that becomes a portfolio platform through plugins (Redmine Portfolio, Redmine Agile, etc.); offers multi-project views, resource heatmaps, and portfolio dashboards with complete customization freedom; requires technical effort to configure but has zero licensing cost .



- **[Taiga](https://github.com/taigaio/taiga)**  

  Agile project management platform (MPL-2.0) supporting Scrum and Kanban across multiple projects; multi-project views, epic hierarchies spanning projects, and cumulative flow/burndown reports provide portfolio visibility for agile-heavy organizations; self-hosted open-source edition is free with unlimited projects .



- **[OrangeScrum Community](https://github.com/Orangescrum/Orangescrum)**  

  Simple open-source PPM tool with resource utilization charts, cross-project Gantt views, time tracking, and invoicing; quick to deploy (PHP/MySQL) and suitable for small to mid-sized PMOs wanting immediate portfolio visibility without complex setup; Community edition free for unlimited users .



- **[LibrePlan](https://github.com/LibrePlan/libreplan)**  

  Web-based project planning tool with a strong resource-constrained scheduling engine; automatically levels resources across projects and supports cross-project dependencies; completely free with no paid tier, though the UI is dated and no agile views are available; best suited for engineering and construction portfolios where resource leveling is the critical challenge .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ProjectLibre** — Open-source desktop project management tool often positioned as an MS Project alternative; useful for traditional plan-driven portfolios, though not natively a multi-project portfolio platform .

- **GLPI with ProjectBridge plugin** — GLPI plugin linking tickets, project tasks, and contracts for time and cost tracking within an IT service management context .

- **OpenProject PMflex** — OpenProject's configuration for the German public sector PMflex standard, offering structured portfolio/program/project governance aligned with federal requirements .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **OpenProject** for full lifecycle portfolio governance and Gantt-based planning, **Leantime** for strategy-to-OKR alignment and impact scoring, **LibrePlan** for algorithmic resource leveling across constrained portfolios, and **Redmine** with portfolio plugins for maximum customization in a fully self-hosted strategic portfolio management stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Strategic portfolio platforms handling financial and strategic data must comply with applicable regulations and organizational governance policies.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, integration planning with ERP/finance systems, and governance workflow customization for portfolio approval gates.

- Verify license terms for commercial use; OpenProject uses GPL-3.0 with paid Enterprise editions, Leantime uses MIT with paid cloud plans, Redmine uses GPL-2.0, and LibrePlan is completely free with no paid tier.
